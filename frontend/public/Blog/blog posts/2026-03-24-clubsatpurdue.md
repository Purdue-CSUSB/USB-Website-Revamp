# Finding Your Place: How Clubs Defined My Purdue Experience

Looking back at my time at Purdue, from freshman year to now, when I am a junior, I can say without a doubt that a lot of my experience has been defined by the clubs I have been involved in. If you're just starting out at Purdue and wondering where to begin, I hope my journey gives you a useful roadmap.

## Getting Started: Finding Your First Club

The first club I joined at Purdue was USB, or the Computer Science Undergraduate Student Board. I had been interested in USB since the time I began researching clubs to explore at Purdue, right after I accepted my admission offer. I was thrilled to join and to contribute to the CS community by seeing how it grows. Around the same time, I also started attending meetings for Gardening Club, which were held at the Asian American and Asian Resource and Cultural Center (AAARCC). Every week in Gardening club, we’d harvest the vegetables and fruits we’d planted, and we’d all cook dinner together and occasionally play Mario Kart afterwards. USB and the Gardening club set the bar for what a good club experience could look like, and it motivated me to join more clubs.

## Stepping Into Professional Development

Joining Purdue Solutions as a sophomore in the fall semester exposed me to new consulting and fintech opportunities. It taught me how to improve my networking skills and converse with recruiters at career fairs. Learning how to case throughout the interview process — and indirectly and directly applying those skills to my interviews — gave me further confidence in a professional sense. Purdue Solutions also helped me understand how to improve my resume and advocate for myself better.

## Growing Into Leadership

I became involved in more leadership roles second semester sophomore year, as a lead for the Blog initiative in USB and as a project manager for Purdue Solutions. These roles helped me improve my organizational and communication skills greatly. Taking on responsibility within clubs you already love is one of the most natural ways to grow.

## Building Something From Scratch

My friends and I cofounded Purdue Stack, a software engineering club, in the summer of 2025 before my junior year started. Building a club from the ground up and seeing incredible reception through application numbers and member feedback has been one of the most fulfilling aspects of my student life at Purdue. If you ever feel like the club you're looking for doesn't exist yet, you can create it pretty easily.

## The Doors Clubs Can Open

Connections created through clubs opened up unexpected doors. Getting involved led me to become a CS ambassador, travel to the Bay Area and San Diego, visit large consulting firms in Chicago, and take a trip to Carmel, Indiana to see the solar eclipse. None of these experiences were things I could have planned for, and they were a lot of fun!

## What I'll Carry Forward

I have found friendship and improved my leadership and professional development skills through clubs at Purdue — skills that I will carry with me throughout my lifetime. Whether you're a freshman just arriving on campus or an upperclassman looking to get more involved, clubs at Purdue have something for everyone.
